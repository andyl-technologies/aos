# CNP construction and preparation custody

The CNP adapter keeps the original transport and native ownership separate from
portable receipts. A parsed response cannot create an activation, an execution
lease, behavioral acceptance or permission to reclaim a process.

## Construction

`CnpController::new_retained` borrows caller-owned session, journal and route
slots. It validates the negotiated registration, selected evidence feature and
finite exchange budget before taking any slot. Refusal leaves all three slots
unchanged. Successful construction moves the same values without an intervening
callback or allocation. The Packet launcher prepares its custody before Child
and installs the session immediately after the original Hello completes.

The Packet collection route retains the original complete collection plan,
source oracle, native peer, journals, case tickets and publishers. Collection
permission remains distinct from ordinary node qualification. Unknown effects
remain held by the original supervisor; collecting a report does not authorize
a replacement peer or a repeated operation.

## Provider preparation

The installed provider registry authenticates its implementation revision and
finite preparation plan. `ProviderWorldPreparation` reserves participant storage
before native construction and owns one original `PreparedRealization`. It
adopts each participant before inspecting its callbacks. Failure or unwind
transfers the complete partial preparation to the pre-reserved world supervisor.

The current installed vendor adapter supports multiple owners of one
implementation. Mixed implementations must contribute to one shared preparation
and pass complete graph admission before common Arm. Independently prepared
worlds cannot be combined by extracting their nodes: their reservations and
supervision slots belong to their original worlds.

## Verified scope

The Packet checkpoint passes fourteen current-source controls, including five
constructor-refusal ownership cases, four-consumer strict compilation and 37
source-quality cases. The vendor checkpoint passes 25 inert ownership and
protocol controls, strict compilation and 37 source-quality cases. Three
native-free failed-retirement readers also pass; the corresponding ignored
end-to-end fixture has not run.

These checks establish the tested API and custody behavior. Complete three-peer
collection, mixed native execution, installed behavioral acceptance, native
shutdown/reaping and exact capture/restore remain separate qualification work.
The prior application test-target gate predates these checkpoints. The final
application, ABI/license and matched Linux performance gates remain required.
Raw test artifacts remain local.
